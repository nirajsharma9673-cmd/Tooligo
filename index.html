Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators accurate?

Yes, all our calculators use proven mathematical formulas and algorithms. They perform calculations locally on your device, ensuring accuracy and privacy.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No, all tools are completely free and require no registration. Simply visit the tool page and start calculating.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We do not store, track, or share your data with anyone.

Can I use these tools on mobile devices?

Absolutely! All our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

Do the tools work offline?

Yes, most tools work offline once the page has loaded. However, the currency converter requires internet connection for live rates.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No. All tools are completely free with no hidden charges, subscriptions, or premium versions.
