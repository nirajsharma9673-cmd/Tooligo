About This Calculator

The Age Calculator is a simple and accurate tool that calculates your exact age based on your birth date. It provides your age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes.

Perfect for finding your exact age for documents, applications, or just for fun!

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the age calculator?

The age calculator is 100% accurate. It calculates your exact age based on your birth date and today's date using precise date calculations.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes, you can calculate anyone's age by entering their date of birth. The calculator works for any date in the past.

Why might my age be off by one day?

Age varies slightly depending on the timezone and time of day. The calculator uses your local timezone for calculations.

Can I see my age in different formats?

Yes! The calculator shows your age in years/months/days format as well as total days, hours, and minutes lived.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No, your birth date is never stored or transmitted. All calculations happen locally in your browser for complete privacy.

What's the earliest date I can enter?

You can enter any date from the past. The calculator handles dates from centuries ago without any issues.
