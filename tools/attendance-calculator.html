How It Works

This calculator compares your attended classes against the total classes held to determine your current attendance percentage. Depending on whether you are above or below the minimum requirement, it tells you what to do next.

Formulas:

  • Current % = (Attended ÷ Total Held) × 100
  • If above minimum: Max skippable = floor( Attended ÷ R − Total ), where R = required % as decimal
  • If below minimum: Classes needed = ceil( (R × Total − Attended) ÷ (1 − R) )

Worked Example: You have attended 60 out of 80 classes and need 75% attendance. Current percentage is 60 ÷ 80 × 100 = 75% . You are right at the threshold. You can skip 0 classes right now — attending the next class keeps you safely above 75%, but missing even one drops you below.

If instead you had attended 55 out of 80 classes, your percentage would be 68.75%. To reach 75% you must attend the next 20 classes in a row. After those 20, total becomes 100 and attended becomes 75, giving exactly 75%.

How to Use

  1. Enter the total number of classes held so far
  2. Enter the number of classes you have attended
  3. Set the required attendance percentage (default 75%)
  4. Click Calculate
  5. See your current percentage, status, and recommended action

Formula

Current Attendance %: (Attended Classes / Total Classes) × 100

Max Classes Skippable (if at or above minimum): floor( Attended / (R) − Total ), where R = Required % ÷ 100

Classes Needed to Recover (if below minimum): ceil( (R × Total − Attended) / (1 − R) )

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 75% the default minimum attendance?

Most Indian colleges and universities mandate a minimum 75% attendance per semester for students to be eligible to appear for final examinations. This rule is set by affiliated universities like Mumbai University, Delhi University, VTU, Anna University, and many others. Some institutions may require a higher percentage, so always check your college's specific policy.

What happens if my attendance falls below the minimum?

Consequences vary by institution. Most commonly, students with insufficient attendance are not allowed to sit for semester-end exams. Some colleges issue warnings, require parents to be notified, or mandate detention classes. In severe cases, students may need to repeat the semester or pay a fine. Some colleges offer a grace period or condonation if the shortfall is small (e.g., 5%).

Does this calculator save or share my data?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server, saved to disk, or shared with third parties. You can refresh the page or close your browser and nothing is retained.

How is the "classes needed to recover" formula derived?

The formula solves for x where (Attended + x) / (Total + x) = R (the required decimal). Rearranging: Attended + x = R(Total + x) → Attended + x = R×Total + R×x → x − R×x = R×Total − Attended → x(1 − R) = R×Total − Attended → x = (R×Total − Attended) / (1 − R). We take the ceiling because you must attend whole classes.

Can I use this for any required percentage?

Yes. The required percentage field is fully adjustable. While 75% is the most common requirement in Indian colleges, you can enter any value from 1% to 99%. This is useful if your institution has a different policy or if you want to set a personal target.
