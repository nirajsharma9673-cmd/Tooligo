Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate discount amount?

Formula: (Original Price × Discount %) / 100. For example, 20% off ₹100 = (100 × 20) / 100 = ₹20 discount.

Can I calculate multiple discounts?

This calculator works for single discounts. For multiple discounts, apply them one after another.

Does this include GST?

No, this calculator shows discount on the price. GST may be applicable separately depending on your location and item type.

Is this accurate for all items?

Yes, the discount calculation formula is universal and works for all types of items and prices.
