How It Works

The discount calculator shows you how much you save when a percentage discount is applied to an item’s original price. It uses a simple two-step formula:

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Worked Example

Scenario: A jacket costs Rs 2,500 and is on sale for 30% off.

Step 1: Discount = 2,500 × (30 ÷ 100) = 2,500 × 0.30 = Rs 750

Step 2: Final Price = 2,500 − 750 = Rs 1,750

You save Rs 750 and pay only Rs 1,750 for the jacket. Use this calculator to quickly compare deals across different stores.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate discount amount?

Formula: (Original Price × Discount %) / 100. For example, 20% off ₹100 = (100 × 20) / 100 = ₹20 discount.

Can I calculate multiple discounts?

This calculator works for single discounts. For multiple discounts, apply them one after another.

Does this include GST?

No, this calculator shows discount on the price. GST may be applicable separately depending on your location and item type.

Is this accurate for all items?

Yes, the discount calculation formula is universal and works for all types of items and prices.
