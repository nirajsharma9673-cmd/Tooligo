What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed amount you pay monthly to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest portions.

Our calculator uses the standard EMI formula to provide accurate results for home loans, car loans, personal loans, and more.

How It Works

The EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is calculated using the standard formula used by banks worldwide. Each EMI payment consists of both principal repayment and interest, structured so that the loan is fully paid off by the end of the tenure.

EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n - 1]

Where P = Loan Amount, r = Monthly Interest Rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = Loan Tenure in Months. The calculator also generates a year-by-year amortization schedule showing how much principal and interest you pay each year.

Worked Example

Scenario: A home loan of Rs 50,00,000 at 8% annual interest for 20 years.

Step 1: Monthly rate r = 8 / 12 / 100 = 0.00667. Number of months n = 20 x 12 = 240.

Step 2: EMI = 50,00,000 x 0.00667 x (1.00667)^240 / [(1.00667)^240 - 1] = Rs 41,822 per month.

Step 3: Total amount payable = 41,822 x 240 = Rs 1,00,37,280. Total interest = 1,00,37,280 - 50,00,000 = Rs 50,37,280.

Use the amortization schedule to see how much principal vs. interest you pay each year. In the early years, a larger portion goes toward interest; over time, more goes toward principal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the formula: EMI = [P * r * (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1], where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of months.

Can this calculator be used for all loan types?

Yes, this EMI calculator works for home loans, car loans, personal loans, education loans, and any other fixed-rate loans.

What's the difference between EMI and total amount?

EMI is your monthly payment, while total amount is EMI multiplied by number of months. Total interest is the difference between total amount and principal.

Is this calculator accurate?

Yes, our calculator uses the exact EMI formula used by banks and financial institutions for precise results.

Can I use this for variable interest rates?

This calculator is designed for fixed interest rates. For variable rates, use the rate you expect for average calculation.

Does this include processing fees?

No, this calculator shows basic EMI. Actual EMI might include processing fees and other charges as per your loan agreement.
