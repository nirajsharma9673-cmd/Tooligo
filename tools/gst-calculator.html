About GST

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax levied on goods and services. This calculator helps you find the tax amount and final price.

How to Use

  1. Enter the price (base or final)
  2. Select the GST rate
  3. Check "Price includes GST" if the entered amount already includes tax
  4. Click Calculate to view base price, GST amount, and final price

GST Rates

  • 5% - Essential goods
  • 12% - Standard rate
  • 18% - Most goods/services
  • 28% - Luxury items

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a comprehensive indirect tax that replaced earlier taxes like VAT and excise duty.

How is GST calculated?

GST is calculated as: GST Amount = (Price × GST Rate) / 100. Final Price = Price + GST Amount.

Do all items have the same GST rate?

No, different items have different GST rates ranging from 0% to 28% based on the nature of goods/services.

Is this calculator accurate?

Yes, it uses the correct GST calculation formula. However, consult official sources for specific items.
