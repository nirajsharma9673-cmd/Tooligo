How It Works

This converter uses the CBSE standard formula where CGPA is calculated by dividing the percentage by 9.5. This 9.5 divisor was determined by the CBSE based on results analysis of previous board exams and is the most widely used conversion standard across Indian schools and universities.

Important caveat: While the 9.5 divisor is the CBSE standard, some universities and colleges use different conversion factors. Always verify your institution's specific formula if you need an official conversion.

Formulas:

  • CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5
  • Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

Worked Examples:

  • A percentage of 85% converts to CGPA = 85 ÷ 9.5 = 8.95
  • A CGPA of 8.2 converts to percentage = 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the 9.5 factor come from?

The CBSE determined the 9.5 conversion factor by analyzing the average marks obtained by top-scoring students across board exams over several years. The 9.5 factor represents the average of marks-to-CGPA correlation and is published in official CBSE guidelines. It has since become the de facto standard used by most Indian schools.

Does this formula apply to all universities?

No. While the 9.5 divisor is the CBSE standard and widely adopted across Indian schools and many universities, some institutions use different conversion factors. For example, Mumbai University uses a different table, and some autonomous colleges define their own conversion. Always check your specific institution's official conversion policy for formal submissions.

How do I convert CGPA back to percentage?

Simply multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, CGPA 7.5 × 9.5 = 71.25%. This calculator does both directions automatically — just type in either field.

Is this conversion officially recognized?

The CBSE formula (divide by 9.5) is officially recognized by CBSE for its own certificates. However, individual institutions may have their own conversion policies. This calculator provides the standard CBSE result and should be verified against your institution's requirements for official use.

Why are results shown to 2 decimal places?

CGPA values are commonly reported to 2 decimal places on official transcripts and mark sheets. Rounding to 2 decimals matches the standard format used by CBSE and most Indian universities for reporting final grades.
