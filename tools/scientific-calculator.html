How It Works

This scientific calculator supports basic arithmetic operations along with advanced mathematical functions. You can use it for:

  • Basic Operations: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentage calculations
  • Trigonometric Functions: Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), and Tangent (tan) for angle calculations in degrees or radians
  • Logarithmic Functions: Base-10 logarithm (log) and natural logarithm (ln) for exponential calculations
  • Power & Roots: Exponentiation (x^y), square root (√), and factorial (n!)
  • Combinatorics: Combinations (nCr) and Permutations (nPr) for probability calculations

Worked Examples

Example 1 (Trigonometry): To find sin(30°), ensure the mode is set to DEG, then press: sin 30 = 0.5.

Example 2 (Logarithm): To find log(100), press: log 100 = 2 (since 10² = 100).

Example 3 (Combinations): To find how many ways to choose 3 items from 7 (7C3), enter N=7, R=3, and click nCr. Result: 35.

Example 4 (Factorial): To find 5!, enter 5 in the factorial field and click Calculate. Result: 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120.

Tips for Use

This calculator follows the standard order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS). Use parentheses by chaining operations sequentially. The display shows your current input and result. Use AC to clear all, ← for backspace, and ± to toggle between positive and negative values.

Scientific Calculator Features

What trigonometric functions are supported?

Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), and Tangent (tan) functions are supported. Always use angles in degrees.

How do I use the square root function?

Click √, then enter the number and press =. For example: √ 16 = 4.

What does x^y do?

It raises x to the power of y. For example: 2 x^y 3 = 8 (2³).

How do I calculate logarithms?

Use 'log' for base-10 logarithm or 'ln' for natural logarithm (base e).

Can I calculate factorial?

Yes, use the n! button or the dedicated Factorial calculator below for larger numbers.
