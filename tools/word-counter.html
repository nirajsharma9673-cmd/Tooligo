How It Works

Every keystroke is analyzed in real time to give you instant text statistics:

  • Word count — Text is split on whitespace; empty strings are discarded.
  • Character count — Counts every character including spaces, and again excluding spaces.
  • Sentence count — Text is split on period, exclamation, and question mark punctuation.
  • Paragraph count — Text is split on double newlines (blank lines between paragraphs).
  • Reading time — Estimated at 200 words per minute (average adult reading speed), rounded up.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the text area
  2. All statistics update automatically as you type
  3. Use Clear to reset the counter
  4. Copy your text elsewhere when done

Counting Methods

Words: Split on whitespace and filter empty strings

Sentences: Split on . ! ? punctuation (heuristic, may not match full NLP parsing)

Reading Time: word count / 200 wpm, minimum "< 1 min"

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool store what I type?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser. No data is sent to any server, saved to disk, or shared with anyone. You can refresh or close the page and everything is gone.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time is calculated by dividing the word count by 200 words per minute, which is the average reading speed for adults. The result is rounded up to the nearest minute, with a minimum of "< 1 min" for very short texts.

Why might the sentence count be approximate?

This counter uses a simple heuristic based on punctuation (. ! ?) to detect sentences. It does not use full natural language processing (NLP), so it may not accurately handle abbreviations, quoted speech, or other edge cases where punctuation appears mid-sentence.

Is there a text length limit?

There is no practical limit. The browser textarea can handle very large blocks of text without issues. All processing happens locally, so performance depends on your device.
